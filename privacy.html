Privacy Policy

How Khela Hobe collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

Khela Hobe ("we", "us", "our") operates a sports ground booking platform that helps players in Bangladesh find, book, and pay for football, cricket, and basketball venues. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, why we collect it, and how it is stored and protected.

By creating an account or using Khela Hobe, you agree to the collection and use of information in line with this policy.

Information We Collect

  • Account details: Name, email address, phone number, and password (stored securely as a hash, never in plain text).
  • Google Sign-In data: If you register or log in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google.
  • Booking information: The grounds you book, dates, time slots, and booking status.
  • Payment details: Payment method chosen (bKash, Nagad, Rocket, card, or cash) and transaction records. We do not permanently store full card numbers or CVV codes.
  • Profile picture: If you choose to upload one.
  • Player-matching data: Match requests you create or join, including sport type and preferred players needed.
  • Usage data: Basic technical information such as browser type and general activity on the platform, used for troubleshooting and improving the service.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account, and let you log in securely.
  • To process bookings, confirm payments, and send booking or payment notifications.
  • To connect you with other players through the player-matching feature.
  • To send important account or booking emails, such as booking confirmations.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access to accounts.
  • To improve our platform based on how it is used.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited information only in these cases:

  • With ground owners: When you book a ground, the owner can see your name and booking details so they can approve or manage the booking.
  • With payment partners: Mobile banking and card details are processed through the payment method you select.
  • With Google: If you use Google Sign-In, authentication is handled by Google in line with their own privacy policy.
  • When required by law: If legally required to do so by a court order or government authority.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect your information, including password hashing and restricted access to our database. However, no method of storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights and Choices

  • You can view and update your name, phone number, password, and profile picture at any time from your Profile page.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us.
  • You can choose not to provide certain optional information, such as a profile picture, though this may limit some features.

Cookies & Sessions

Khela Hobe uses session cookies to keep you logged in while you browse the site. These are essential for the platform to function and are cleared when you log out or close your browser session.

Children's Privacy

Khela Hobe is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. Continued use of Khela Hobe after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, reach out to us at info@khelahobee.com or visit our Contact Us page.
