❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a ground?

Login → Search for a ground → Click "Book Now" → Confirm Booking.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes, you can cancel bookings up to 24 hours before the scheduled time.

How do I contact the ground owner?

After booking, you'll receive the owner's contact information in your confirmation.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept cash and online payment through mobile banking (bKash, Nagad, Rocket).
