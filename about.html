About Khela Hobe

Bangladesh's first sports aggregator platform.

Our Mission

Khela Hobe is Bangladesh's first sports aggregator platform. We help players discover and book football, cricket, and basketball turfs with just a few clicks — making sports accessible to everyone.

Our Vision

To become the leading sports booking platform in South Asia, empowering athletes and sports enthusiasts to find and book the best playing fields effortlessly.

Our Values

  • Quality Assurance: We ensure all listed turfs meet high standards of maintenance and safety.
  • Speed & Efficiency: Quick booking process with instant confirmations.
  • Trust & Reliability: Building long-term relationships with our users and partners.
  • Innovation: Continuously improving our platform with new features.
  • Community: Fostering a vibrant sports community across Bangladesh.

Our Impact

50+ Playgrounds
100+ Customers
80+ Bookings
10+ Cities

Our Story

Founded in June 2026 during the FIFA World Cup, Khela Hobe was born from a passion for sports and a vision to connect players with the best playing fields across Bangladesh. What started as a small idea has grown into a trusted platform for athletes and sports enthusiasts.
